- Arguments and logical analysis (Summer 2025)

    The second iteration of an introductory and interdisciplinary critical thinking course intended for advanced high-school students and undergraduates. I collaborated with my comrade Philip Sink to design and refine the syllabus, content and teaching material. The first half of the course covered introductory logic, probability theory and statistics, while the second half applied these basic formal tools to understand and evaluate arguments drawn from a range of fields.
